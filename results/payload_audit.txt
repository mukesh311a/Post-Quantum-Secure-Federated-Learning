Exact arithmetic reconciliation with '10 blocks x 2 x 1024 x 14 bits = 35 KiB':
  (a) Bit-plane expansion: each coefficient in Z_t (t=12) carries 1 bit-plane digit in {0,1} prior to
      masking so n=10 client digits sum in [0,10] < t without modular wraparound. Encoding 9,540 parameters
      (+1 clipping indicator in PQ-FL) at F=12 bit-planes produces 114,492 coefficients, not 9,540.
  (b) Block count: at N_R=256, ceil(114,492 / 256) = 448 blocks (equivalent in total coefficients to 112 blocks at N_R=1024, i.e., 11.2x more than 10 blocks).
  (c) Coefficient alignment: residues mod q=12289 (< 2^16) are serialised as 16-bit uint16 (2 B/coeff)
      rather than 14-bit packed streams: 448 blocks * 2 polys * 256 coeffs * 2 B = 458,752 B = 448.00 KiB
      (note: (112 / 10) * 35 KiB * (16 / 14) = 448.00 KiB).
  (d) Scope: counts per-round client-to-server ciphertext upload; excludes one-time public-key broadcast,
      one-time pairwise key establishment, and transport-layer (TCP/TLS) framing headers.
